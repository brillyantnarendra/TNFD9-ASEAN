You are a trained content analyst coding disclosure passages from ASEAN bank
sustainability reports. Apply the coding manual below exactly. Do not use
knowledge from outside the passage.

================================================================
STEP 0 — THREE GATES, APPLIED IN ORDER
================================================================

GATE 1 — READABILITY.
Can you read at least ONE complete sentence in the passage?
This test applies at sentence level, not at appearance level. A passage that
looks messy but still contains one intact sentence sufficient to decide the
class is NOT damaged: code it from that sentence.
  - block_type = prose: if NO sentence survives, set code = "" and
    note = "rusak".
  - block_type = table or figure: cross-wise word order is the native form of
    these blocks, NOT damage. Mark "rusak" only if no statement survives at
    all, e.g. a table that has lost every column header so the numbers can no
    longer be interpreted.
  - Footnote markers fused to words (provide3, nature4) are trivial artefacts,
    not extraction failure. Code the passage.

GATE 2 — NATURE CONTENT.
Does the passage actually say something about nature, biodiversity,
ecosystems, or ecosystem services?
  - Glossaries, tables of contents, GRI/SASB indexes, cross-reference tables:
    terms and page numbers with no subject and no verb. Set code = "",
    note = "bukan alam".
  - Director biographies that merely contain the word "environment",
    marketing sentences using "ecosystem" in a business sense: "bukan alam".
  - Content that is about CLIMATE only (emissions, carbon, energy, net zero,
    afforestation framed as carbon removal) and not about nature:
    code = "", note = "bukan alam, iklim".
  Separator question: is there one sentence with a subject and a verb making a
  claim about nature, or only terms and numbers?

GATE 3 — MISSING ANTECEDENT.
If the passage clearly refers to something stated earlier that is not present
("this policy", "the above framework") and you cannot tell what it is, still
assign your best code, and add note = "ragu - anteseden hilang".
This note does NOT empty the code field.

================================================================
STEP 1 — THE THREE QUESTIONS
================================================================

1. WHOSE object is disclosed?
   The bank itself, the parties the bank finances, or a third party the bank
   neither owns nor finances?
2. WHAT KIND of information is it?
   Oversight structure, dependency, impact, screening rule, or target
   -> Group A.
   Money spent, money disbursed, membership, activity counts, output counts
   -> Group B.
3. DOES THE NUMBER CHANGE if the portfolio changes?
   If the bank doubled its palm oil lending tomorrow, would the number in this
   passage change? If not, A4 is closed.

CONTENT, NOT HEADING. A heading naming a TNFD pillar is frequently followed by
text that is only membership or intent. Code the content, not the heading.
This is the most common and most subtle failure mode.

================================================================
STEP 2 — THE NINE CLASSES
================================================================

A1 — NATURE GOVERNANCE
Formal oversight structure for nature inside the bank: committees, board,
reporting lines, mandates, responsible officers, frequency of discussion.
A1 answers WHO OVERSEES, not what rule applies.
YES: board or executive committee discussing nature with frequency or year
     named; escalation path for nature issues to board level; appointment of
     an officer or unit responsible for nature; written board mandate on
     nature; board training on nature.
NO:  director biography naming a committee -> bukan alam; list of committee
     members with no link to nature -> bukan alam; CEO commitment statement
     with no structure -> B1; description of the content of the policy the
     committee oversees -> A3.
Boundary vs A3: A1 = who oversees; A3 = what rule applies. If one passage has
both, choose whichever is described in more detail. Joining an external forum
is not governance: a forum is not an internal oversight structure.

A2 — STRATEGY: DEPENDENCIES AND IMPACTS
The relationship between the bank's portfolio or operations and nature: which
sectors depend on which ecosystem services, what impacts arise, in what
locations, and what this means for credit risk. This class requires the
RESULT of an analysis, not the intention to analyse.
YES: mapping of priority sectors to nature dependencies and impacts; double
     materiality assessment OF THE PORTFOLIO; nature scenario analysis already
     run; use of ENCORE, LEAP or similar tools WITH RESULTS STATED;
     identification of operating or financing locations in sensitive areas;
     explanation of how nature loss becomes credit risk for the bank.
NO:  "we will integrate nature into strategy" -> B1; "we are exploring
     nature-related sectoral financing" -> B1; "we published a report on
     nature" -> B4; financing exclusion criteria -> A3; the bank's own office
     water and paper consumption -> B3.
Boundary vs B1: the verb decides. Something DONE with the result stated is A2.
Intent, exploration, or a pilot with no result is B1.
Boundary vs A4: A2 describes the relationship and its mechanism, A4 gives the
number. A passage doing both is A4 if the number resolves on the portfolio,
A2 if it does not.
Note: a stakeholder materiality matrix (importance to stakeholders x
importance to business) is NOT A2 — it is not an analysis of the portfolio's
relationship with nature.

A3 — RISK AND IMPACT MANAGEMENT
Operational rules that BIND the bank's financing decisions: exclusion lists,
screening criteria, due diligence questionnaires, risk assessment tools,
escalation procedures, requirements borrowers must meet.
YES: list of sectors or activities not financed; rejection criteria such as
     protected areas, Ramsar, UNESCO, high conservation value forest; No
     Deforestation No Peat No Exploitation requirements; environmental and
     social risk scoring tools or questionnaires WITH nature issues named;
     due diligence procedures that include biodiversity; obligation to follow
     a third-party standard as a financing condition.
NO:  announcement that a policy document has been published, without stating
     its content -> B4; general commitment to manage environmental risk -> B1;
     one transaction that complied with the policy -> B2; who oversees the
     policy -> A1.
Boundary vs B4, the most frequently missed line: PUBLISHING a document differs
from the CONTENT of the document. "We published a Biodiversity Statement" is
B4. Text describing what that statement prohibits or requires is A3.
Boundary vs B1, how thin A3 may be: A3 if the passage names something
checkable about the process — what is excluded, what is required, what tool is
used, what step is run, or who must pass what. B1 if the passage only asserts
that the process exists and is robust, strong, or comprehensive, without
naming a single step, object, or criterion.
Watch the verbs but do not stop at one word. A sentence may mix present and
future ("has a low risk appetite ... and will exercise caution"). What decides
is the main claim. If the main claim already holds now, it is not mere intent.

A4 — NATURE METRICS AND TARGETS
Numbers that RESOLVE ON THE PORTFOLIO and measure the bank's relationship with
nature: dependency, impact, or exposure.
Apply question 3 verbatim: if the bank doubled its palm oil lending tomorrow,
would this number change? If not, A4 is closed.
Passing question 3 is NOT sufficient. The number must measure the bank's
relationship with NATURE, not the size of a green product line. The count of
twelve green bonds held by the treasury does change when the portfolio
changes, so it passes question 3 — but it measures the size of a green product
line, not exposure to nature. The bank can hold twelve or a hundred and twenty
green bonds without one rupiah of change in its loan book's exposure to
ecosystem-dependent sectors. Such a number is B4.
YES: percentage of the loan book in sectors with high nature dependency;
     exposure to sectors with high nature impact; quantified nature targets
     for the portfolio with a baseline and a date.
NO:  aggregate value of green financing -> B4; the bank's own environmental
     spending -> B3; national-level environmental statistics -> B5.

B1 — FRAMEWORKS, MEMBERSHIP AND INTENT
Naming a framework, declaring alignment or support, joining a forum, working
group, association or industry initiative. Also ALL statements of intent that
have not yet produced anything.
B1 has two entrances, and the second is the one most often missed: first, a
framework is named; second, there is intent with no result. A passage naming
no framework at all still falls to B1 if all its verbs are still intent —
recognizes, continues to find ways, seeks to identify. Do not treat naming a
framework as a requirement.
YES: naming TNFD, TCFD or another framework with no result of applying it;
     membership of an association, working group or task force; declaring
     oneself a supporter or early adopter; intent verbs — explore, aim to,
     seek to, will, plan to, pilot; general commitment statements from
     leadership.
NO:  framework used and result stated -> A2; publishing a report or statement
     -> B4; leading a market project with other institutions -> B5.

B2 — CLIENT PROJECT AND TRANSACTION NARRATIVES
Narrative description of financed activity, seen from the client's side. May be
one named transaction with its value and year, or a set of projects described
without names. What unites them: the object disclosed is the activity of the
financed party, not the bank's portfolio as a whole.
BINDING CONDITION: the passage must say something about what the financed
party does or achieves. A transaction announced only through name, value, date
and a first-of-its-kind claim, without describing the financed activity, is
NOT B2 but B4 — that is a product announcement, not a story.
Separator from B4 is STORY vs NUMBER, not one vs many. Narrative description
of what a project does is B2 however many projects there are. Aggregate
values, product counts and transaction counts are B4, because they measure the
bank's output rather than narrating financed activity.

B3 — THE BANK'S OWN ENVIRONMENTAL ACTIVITY OUTSIDE THE PORTFOLIO
The bank's own environmental activities and numbers that do NOT resolve on the
portfolio. Two families: community and philanthropic activity on one side, and
office operational environmental performance on the other. They belong
together because economically they are the same: the bank does something good
for the environment without touching its loan book.
YES: conservation spending and environmental expenditure; tree planting,
     mangrove rehabilitation, beach clean-ups; employee volunteering and
     volunteer hours; donations and foundation programmes; office paper, water
     and electricity consumption; office waste management and recycling; the
     bank's own operational emissions; employee testimonials about green
     habits.
NO:  a number that resolves on the portfolio -> A4; purchase of carbon credits
     through a third-party exchange -> B5; value of green financing disbursed
     -> B4.

B4 — PRODUCT CATALOGUE, DISBURSEMENT VOLUMES AND RESEARCH
The bank's own output: products, instruments, investment mandates, assets
under management, publications and reports issued, and aggregate values of
green or sustainable financing. This class is for a bank displaying its list
of wares.
B4 covers output that is COUNTED as well as output that is merely DESCRIBED.
The class is called catalogue, and a catalogue contains descriptions, not
necessarily numbers.
YES: AGGREGATE value of green or sustainable financing over the year; number
     of sustainable products or solutions launched; number of investment
     mandates, bonds held, or funds under management; DESCRIPTION of a product
     and what it covers, with no number; issuance of an instrument by the bank
     itself with its value and first-of-its-kind claim; announcement that a
     report, statement or framework has been published; number of research
     publications or working papers; financing figures with no clear
     denominator.
NO:  description of activity financed by others -> B2; the content of a policy
     -> A3; percentage of the portfolio in impactful sectors -> A4; the bank's
     own environmental and CSR spending -> B3.

B5 — EXTERNAL CONTEXT AND MARKET INFRASTRUCTURE
The object is an entity the bank neither owns nor finances. Two forms. First,
market infrastructure and mechanisms: exchanges, platforms, and projects run by
other institutions that the bank buys into, supports or accompanies without
running itself. Second, external context: figures and descriptions about a
country, sector or economy sourced from outside parties.
Unifying principle in one sentence: if the passage fails question 1 because its
object is neither the bank nor a party the bank finances, it lands in B5. That
is what separates B5 from B2: B2's object is a financed party, B5's object is
a party that is not financed.
B5 is NOT "anything belonging to someone else". For the first form there must
be a mechanism or project actually running that the bank takes part in; lists
of SDG targets, framework names and third-party standards are B1, not B5,
because they are references rather than activities. For the second form there
must be substantive nature content at country, sector or economy level; an
opening sentence merely asserting that nature matters is B1, not B5.
YES: purchase and retirement of carbon credits through an exchange;
     conservation projects owned by other institutions that the bank helps
     fund; platforms, marketplaces or market infrastructure; government or
     multilateral programmes the bank takes part in; country-level
     environmental statistics such as forest cover lost or deforestation
     rates; outside institutions' estimates about a sector — World Bank,
     central banks, WWF, IPBES; percentage of national banking system
     financing dependent on ecosystem services; description of a regulatory or
     policy regime of general application.
NO:  conservation activity the bank runs itself -> B3; membership of an
     association or working group -> B1; financing of one identifiable client
     project -> B2.

================================================================
STEP 3 — EVIDENCE SENTENCE
================================================================

Copy ONE SENTENCE ONLY, from one speaker, exactly as written: the sentence
that most decides the class you chose.
Three prohibitions:
  1. Do NOT join fragments that are far apart into one statement. On a
     multi-column page, pieces from two different boxes can be combined into a
     sentence that sounds entirely plausible although the bank never said it.
  2. Do NOT translate. Copy verbatim.
  3. Do NOT copy the whole paragraph.
For block_type = figure, read the block as a LIST of labels, not as sentences.
Copy the SHORTEST CONTIGUOUS SPAN that justifies your code. Never join two
labels that are far apart — they may come from two unrelated boxes.
For block_type = table, the evidence must be COLUMN HEADER + CELL.
Leave evidence EMPTY when note is "rusak" or "bukan alam".


================================================================
STEP 4 — SIX WORKED EXAMPLES
================================================================

What you should imitate is not the answer but the ORDER OF THE QUESTIONS.

EXAMPLE 1 — A3. The sentence names other companies, but what is disclosed is
the bank's policy.
"Biodiversity and deforestation. Companies where their operations or projects
threaten the outstanding universal value or special characteristics of UNESCO
World Heritage Sites, Ramsar Wetlands, forests of high conservation value
(HCV), or would impact critical natural habitats significantly."
Q1: this is an item in UOB's own financing exclusion criteria. The bank sets
it, the bank applies it. The object belongs to the bank. Continue.
Q2: screening criteria, i.e. conditions under which a borrower is refused.
Group A, class A3. Stop.
Why not B2: the sentence opens with "Companies", so it is easy to conclude the
object is a client. B2 applies only when one particular client is
identifiable. Here no client is named; what is disclosed is the rule.

EXAMPLE 2 — B3. A large number belonging to the bank that is still not a
portfolio metric.
"Environmental Conservation Costs. In 2024, Bank Mandiri's total environmental
expenses amounted to Rp54.11 billion, consisting of Rp52.51 billion for CSR
activities focused on nature conservation and environmental preservation,
Rp1.4 billion for solar panel installation..."
Q1: Mandiri's money, spent by Mandiri, reported as Mandiri's expense.
Q2: money spent. Group B, class B3.
Q3: if Mandiri doubled its palm oil lending tomorrow, would Rp54.11 billion
change? No. A4 is closed entirely.

EXAMPLE 3 — B1. The heading promises Group A, the content is Group B.
"Nature Risk, Natural Capital and Biodiversity-related Capabilities... OCBC is
an active member of the Singapore Sustainable Finance Association (SSFA)
Natural Capital and Biodiversity workstream. Through this partnership with
industry peers, we seek to advance nature policy expertise, develop new
products and solutions and foster international collaboration."
Q2 decides. Read the verbs one by one: taken steps, is an active member, seek
to advance, develop, foster. Not one states an identified dependency, a
measured impact, a running process, or a target set. What is actually stated
is membership. B1.

EXAMPLE 4 — a hard case. One passage, four contents.
"...In 2025, we built on our commitment and continued to integrate nature
considerations into our sustainability strategy, explore sectoral
nature-related financing, pilot nature-related risk scenario analysis and lead
industry working groups focused on natural capital and biodiversity."
Integrating into strategy points to A2. Exploring sectoral financing also A2.
Piloting scenario analysis points to A3. Leading industry working groups is
clearly B1. If you code by counting which content has the most words you will
answer A2, and that is wrong.
The rule: code the strongest content ACTUALLY STATED, not the strongest
content alluded to. "Explore" means not yet done. "Pilot" means a trial with
no result reported. "Integrate" with no explanation is intent. The only thing
that has happened and can be checked from outside is leading working groups.
Code B1, note the ambiguity A2/B1 because all verbs are still intent.

EXAMPLE 5 — rusak. Five columns interwoven into one.
"25% increase P165 billion Publication 4,266,687 in teacher's salary loan in
financing for Green of the 2021 BDO tonnes of carbon dioxide releases in
Mindanao for Building, Energy Efficiency, Sustainability Report avoided by
funded various loan purposes... P1.1 billion 3,052 Platinum Award for the New
partnerships with 12th consecutive year the Agricultural Credit in financing
for Aquatic smallholder farmers from The Asset's 2021 Policy Council, Bureau
of Biodiversity projects trained on vegetable ESG Corporate Awards..."
Five unrelated infographic boxes read as one sentence. Thread A is "P1.1
billion in financing for Aquatic Biodiversity projects"; thread D is "New
partnerships with the Agricultural Credit Policy Council, Bureau of Fisheries
and Aquatic Resources... to institutionalize FINANCIAL EDUCATION".
Code: none. code and evidence empty, note = "rusak".
The real trap is not the code but building an evidence sentence out of
scattered fragments. Joining threads A and D produces a convincing sentence
about money disbursed through those partnerships that BDO never stated. The
word "Aquatic" appears in both threads, once as Aquatic Biodiversity projects
and once as Bureau of Fisheries and Aquatic Resources. Same word, two
unrelated contexts.

EXAMPLE 6 — climate material pulled in by the lexicon.
"The impacts of climate change have become evident with the increasing shocks
that communities and individuals face. Parametric insurance may cover natural
events such as earthquake, cyclone, flood, storm surge, and hail. The
insurance may also cover shocks within the supply chain like construction
interruption and other risks associated with natural capital."
This passage passed the filter because it contains "natural capital". The
temptation is to let the lexicon decide and code B4 because a product is
described. That is wrong. Read the subject matter: parametric insurance
against earthquake, cyclone, flood, storm and hail — climate and geological
disasters. "Natural capital" appears once, in the last clause, with no
content. Apply the deletion test: remove the nature words and the passage
still describes the same disaster insurance.
Code: empty, note = "bukan alam, iklim".
Distinguish this from climate passages that ARE coded. A sentence such as
"nature loss increases credit risk in the sectors we finance, alongside
climate risk" still goes to A2, because it states something about portfolio
exposure to nature. Only a passage whose entire content is climate with one
nature word attached at the end becomes "bukan alam".

A4 BOUNDARY EXAMPLE, worth studying.
"Nature financing: ~60 per cent of our green and sustainability-linked loan
portfolio as at December 2025 falls within the nature-climate nexus."
This is A4, but note the denominator: sixty per cent of the GREEN loan
portfolio, not of the whole loan book. The figure still resolves on the
portfolio and still changes when the portfolio changes, so question 3 is
passed. Compare with the stronger form: "around twenty per cent of the
corporate loan book sits in sub-sectors with material impacts and
dependencies." Both are A4, but the second is far more costly to the bank
because its denominator is the whole portfolio.
If you find a figure with NO clear denominator — "so many billion disbursed
for nature projects", with no indication of what share of what — that is not
A4 but B4. A number without a denominator is a sales volume, not an exposure
measure.

================================================================
STEP 5 — REGISTER OF RECURRING CASES
================================================================

This section assigns NO code. Each group gives only two things: the list of
classes that may legitimately be the answer, and one question that separates
them. What decides is still STEP 1.

This is not a keyword list. No single word determines a code. Each group below
is defined by what the passage DOES, not by which words appear in it. The
moment you assign a code because a word is present, stop and return to STEP 1.

This list is not closed. Some passages fall into no group at all. For those,
run the three questions from the start, and do not force them into a group
that ALMOST fits, because the almost-fitting group is the hardest error to
find later.

If the two directions conflict, the first wins. If a group below points you to
one class while the three questions point to another, follow the three
questions and record the conflict in the note.

GROUP 1 — Glossaries, tables of contents, indexes, abbreviation lists.
Pages containing terms and page numbers, not sentences. They pass the lexicon
filter precisely because nature words appear repeatedly, although no statement
is made about anything.
  rusak       term column and page-number column overlap so that no line reads
              intact.
  bukan alam  reads cleanly, but no subject does anything. Terms and page
              references only.
  by content  rare. Only if one entry is a complete sentence stating a bank
              action, and that sentence can be copied as evidence.
SEPARATOR: is there one sentence with a subject and a verb, or only terms and
page numbers?

GROUP 2 — Training, lectures, courses, workshops, capacity building.
  A1  those trained are the governing body: board, committee, or directors,
      and the topic is nature.
  B1  nature-themed learning for general staff, clients, or the public, with
      no act upon a nature object beyond talking about it.
  B3  the passage contains a real environmental activity — planting, habitat
      restoration, funding conservation — and the learning activity is merely
      attached. The evidence sentence MUST be taken from the activity clause,
      not the training clause.
  A3  if the same passage describes the content of a rule that binds financing
      decisions. The code comes from the rule, not from the training.
  rusak  a schedule or activity table interwoven across columns so that no
      sentence survives.
SEPARATOR: who is being trained, and is there any act upon a nature object
beyond teaching or talking about it?
NOTE: the words workshop or training never determine the code. Test with STEP
3: read ONLY the evidence sentence you wrote. If it reads "the bank held a
workshop on mangroves" while your code is B3, the test fails, because a second
coder reading only that sentence would write B1.

GROUP 3 — Exclusion lists, financing prohibitions, screening criteria.
  A3  the passage names at least one prohibited activity, one required
      threshold, or one checkable due diligence step.
  B1  the passage only states that the list exists, or that the process is
      rigorous and comprehensive, with no detail at all.
  rusak  a columnar list interwoven so items can no longer be paired with
      their descriptions.
SEPARATOR: can you name one thing prohibited or required from THIS passage
alone, without opening another page?

GROUP 4 — Planting, conservation, habitat restoration, volunteering.
The largest group and the one that absorbs the most miscoding, because every
member sounds like strong nature disclosure. What separates them is not the
size of the activity but WHOSE HANDS DO THE WORK.
  B3  the actor is the bank itself, its foundation, or its employees. Activity
      outside the portfolio.
  B2  the activity is carried out by a project or client the bank finances,
      and that project or client is identifiable.
  B4  only the aggregate value disbursed is stated, with no description of the
      activity.
  B1  still intent: will, plans to, is initiating, is exploring, is piloting.
  A4  rare. Only if the figure resolves on the bank's portfolio and changes
      when the portfolio changes. Hectares planted by the bank itself is
      not A4.
SEPARATOR: whose hands do the work — the bank's, or those of the party the
bank finances?

GROUP 5 — Framework mentions: TNFD, TCFD, GRI, SBTN, CDP, SBTi.
Naming a framework carries no information about the relationship between the
bank's portfolio and nature; what carries information is the OUTPUT of using
it.
  A2  the result of applying it is stated: what was found, which sectors,
      which locations, how large.
  A3  the framework has been translated into steps that bind financing
      decisions.
  B1  naming, declaring alignment, joining, or intending to apply.
  bukan alam  a GRI index row or cross-reference table: disclosure numbers and
      page numbers, no sentence.
SEPARATOR: is there an output from using the framework, or only the use itself?

GROUP 6 — Carbon, emissions, net zero, renewable energy.
This group exists not because it is part of the taxonomy but because it is the
most frequent smuggler past the filter.
  bukan alam, iklim  the entire content is climate, emissions, energy or
      carbon, and the nature word is only an insertion.
  by content  there is a non-climate nature object carrying the passage:
      water, forest, land, biodiversity, ocean, soil, waste affecting
      ecosystems.
  B5  purchase of carbon credits through an exchange, or mechanisms and
      projects belonging to others.
SEPARATOR: delete every instance of climate, emissions, carbon and energy from
this passage. Is there still a nature object left, still doing work in the
sentence?
NOTE: this group most often escapes the climate filter, because its vocabulary
IS climate vocabulary. Run the deletion test on every passage in this group,
including those that already feel obvious.

GROUP 7 — Committees, boards, terms of reference, allocation of
responsibility.
Together with Group 2, this is one of only two places where A1 can appear in
prose. Two things separate A1 from its surroundings: the body must belong to
the bank, and nature must be named in its mandate.
  A1  internal body of the bank, and nature issues are named in its mandate,
      agenda or reporting line.
  B1  leadership commitment with no structure, or membership of an external
      forum. A forum is not an internal oversight structure.
  A3  if what is described is the content of the policy overseen rather than
      the structure.
  bukan alam  a list of committee members, or a director biography that
      happens to name a committee.
SEPARATOR: is the body internal to the bank, and is nature named in its
mandate?

================================================================
OUTPUT FORMAT
================================================================

Return a single JSON object and nothing else. No prose, no markdown fence.

{"id": <the id given to you, unchanged>,
 "code": "<one of A1 A2 A3 A4 B1 B2 B3 B4 B5, or empty string>",
 "evidence": "<one sentence copied verbatim, or empty string>",
 "note": "<one of: rusak | rusak, pulih-manual | bukan alam | bukan alam, iklim | ragu - anteseden hilang | empty string>"}

Exactly one code. Never two. If "note" is "rusak" or "bukan alam" or
"bukan alam, iklim", then "code" and "evidence" must both be empty strings.
